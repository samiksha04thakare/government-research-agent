## Section 1: Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)
1. Scheme Objective:
   - Direct income support of Rs 6,000 per financial year to all landholding farmer families across India.
2. Disbursement Mechanism:
   - Transferred directly into bank accounts (DBT) in three equal 4-monthly installments of Rs 2,000 each:
     - Period 1: April to July
     - Period 2: August to November
     - Period 3: December to March
3. Mandatory Compliance for Installment Release:
   - Aadhaar-seeding with bank account (DBT enabled via NPCI mapper).
   - Land seeding verification on the PM-KISAN portal by the respective State Revenue Department.
   - Completion of e-KYC (via OTP on PM-KISAN portal, facial recognition on PM-KISAN mobile app, or biometric authentication at Common Service Centres - CSCs).
4. Exclusion Criteria:
   - Institutional landholders.
   - Farmer families holding constitutional posts, former/present ministers, MPs/MLAs, mayors, and district panchayat chairpersons.
   - Serving or retired officers and employees of central/state government and PSUs (excluding Class IV / multi-tasking staff).
   - All persons who paid Income Tax in the last assessment year.
   - Professionals (Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants, Architects) registered with professional bodies.

## Section 2: Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
1. Scheme Objective: Comprehensive crop insurance covering yield loss and localized disasters from pre-sowing to post-harvest.
2. Premium Rates Payable by Farmers:
   - Kharif Crops (Food grains & Oilseeds): Maximum 2.0% of Sum Insured.
   - Rabi Crops (Food grains & Oilseeds): Maximum 1.5% of Sum Insured.
   - Annual Commercial / Horticultural Crops: Maximum 5.0% of Sum Insured.
   - Balance premium is shared equally (50:50) by the Central and State Governments (90:10 in North Eastern States).
3. Coverage Stages & Risk Types:
   - Prevented Sowing / Planting Risk: Up to 25% of sum insured if adverse weather prevents sowing.
   - Standing Crop Yield Loss: Based on Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs) comparing actual yield against threshold yield.
   - Localized Calamities: Hailstorm, landslide, inundation, cloud burst, and natural fire.
   - Post-Harvest Losses: Coverage up to 14 days after harvesting for crops kept in 'cut and spread' condition in the field against unseasonal cyclonic rains/hailstorm.
4. Mandatory 72-Hour Claim Intimation Rule:
   - In case of localized calamity or post-harvest loss, the farmer must report the crop loss within 72 hours via the Crop Insurance App, toll-free helpline (14447), or to the bank / agriculture officer.

## Section 3: PM-KUSUM (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan)
1. Component B (Stand-alone Solar Agriculture Pumps):
   - Supports individual farmers for installation of stand-alone solar agriculture pumps up to 7.5 HP capacity.
   - Subsidy Structure:
     - Central Financial Assistance (CFA): 30% of benchmark cost.
     - State Government Subsidy: Minimum 30% of benchmark cost.
     - Farmer's Contribution: Remaining 40% (can be financed through bank loan up to 30%, meaning farmer pays only 10% upfront).
     - In North Eastern states, hilly and island territories, CFA is 50%, state subsidy 30%, farmer pays 20%.

## Section 4: Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM)
1. Farm Machinery Subsidies:
   - Individual Farmers: Financial assistance of 40% to 50% of the cost of tractor, power tiller, rotavator, laser land leveler, and combine harvesters.
   - Special Beneficiary Norms: Women farmers, SC/ST, and small/marginal farmers receive higher subsidy (up to 50%), while other general farmers receive up to 40%.
   - Custom Hiring Centres (CHC): 40% financial assistance (up to Rs 10 lakh to Rs 25 lakh) for setting up farm machinery banks/CHCs to enable affordable equipment rental for smallholder farmers.
