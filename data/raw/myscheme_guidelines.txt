# myScheme: National Government Platform for Citizen Benefits
Authority: Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) & National e-Governance Division (NeGD)
Source URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in
Publication Date: 2025-01-15
Document Type: Official Guideline

## 1. Overview
myScheme is a flagship national digital platform developed by the Government of India under Digital India. It serves as a unified discovery and eligibility evaluation engine for citizens to access central and state government welfare schemes without having to visit multiple department portals.

## 2. Key Features
- Citizen Eligibility Assessment: Citizens input demographic data such as age, gender, state, caste, and annual household income to identify matched benefits.
- Single Sign-On (SSO): Integrates with MeriPehchaan (National Single Sign-On) and DigiLocker for document verification.
- Multilingual Interface: Available in Hindi, English, and major scheduled Indian languages.

## 3. Scope of Services
myScheme covers over 1,500 Central and State Government schemes across 14 diverse domains:
- Agriculture, Rural & Environment
- Banking, Financial Services & Insurance
- Education & Learning (Scholarships, Fellowships)
- Health & Wellness (Ayushman Bharat, PM-JAY)
- Housing & Shelter (PMAY)
- Social Welfare & Empowerment
- Skills & Employment

## 4. Application and Verification Procedure
1. Citizen fills out the guided 6-step questionnaire on https://www.myscheme.gov.in.
2. The platform generates an eligibility report with list of eligible welfare schemes.
3. Citizen selects the desired scheme and submits required documents via DigiLocker.
4. Final sanction and Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) is disbursed directly to the Aadhaar-seeded bank account by the respective administrative ministry.
